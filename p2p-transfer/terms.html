2. What Oxfer is

  • Oxfer sends files from the sender's device to the recipient's device. When you share, the app creates a link. The recipient opens the link and the two devices connect.
  • Files are encrypted between the two devices. When the devices cannot connect directly, the encrypted data passes through our relay, which cannot read it.
  • Oxfer does not store files on a server. The sender must keep Oxfer open and connected until the recipient has finished downloading. If the sender closes the app or loses the connection, the transfer stops.
  • The link works like a key. Anyone who has the complete link can download the shared files for as long as the sender keeps sharing. Only the sender can end a share, and ending it ends it for everyone who has the link.
  • We cannot see your files, recover them if they are lost, or delete them from anyone's device.
  • Oxfer is free. There are no accounts.
  • The Oxfer software is open source under the MIT License or the Apache License 2.0, at your choice. Those licences govern the source code. These terms govern the service we run at oxfer.app and relay.oxfer.app.

5. How we protect people from illegal content

Oxfer is designed so that content is sent only to people the sender chooses. There is no search, no public directory, no feed, no profiles and no public pages. Files exist on the network only while the sender is actively sharing. We never hold a copy.

Because files are encrypted between the two devices and never pass through us unencrypted, we cannot inspect them. We therefore use no proactive technology, such as automated scanning, hash matching or content classifiers. A person reviews every report we receive.

5.1 Terrorism content

Terrorism content is prohibited. Such content cannot be found through Oxfer by anyone who does not already have a link. When we learn that a share is used for terrorism content, we block it at our relay without delay (section 7) and inform the competent authorities.

5.2 Child sexual exploitation and abuse content

Child sexual abuse material and any content that exploits children is prohibited. When we learn that a share is used for such content, we block it at our relay without delay, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to Romanian authorities, and block the network addresses involved where we know them.

5.3 Other illegal content

All other illegal content is prohibited. When we learn that a share is used for illegal content, we block it at our relay and inform the competent authorities where the law requires it.

5.4 How long illegal content can be present, and how we take it down

A share lasts only while the sender keeps it open. It ends when the sender stops sharing, closes the app or loses the connection. When we block a share at our relay, new connections to it through the relay stop. Browsers need our relay to connect, so in the browser app a block ends the share for new recipients. A block cannot stop a transfer that is already running on a direct connection, a direct connection between two desktop apps, or copies that recipients have already saved. We act on reports within the times given on the abuse and safety page.

6. Reports, complaints and appeals

Email abuse@oxfer.app to:

  • report illegal content or a breach of these terms;
  • complain that we are not following these terms or the law, including our duties on freedom of expression and privacy;
  • appeal a block you believe is wrong.

A person reads every message. We acknowledge within 24 hours and give our answer within 15 days. We decide appeals promptly. If we find that a block was wrong, we lift it. We never tell the person who shared the content, or any other user, that a report was made or who made it, and you can ask us to send you no further messages about your report. The abuse and safety page explains what to include, what not to send, who learns about a report, and faster handling for urgent cases.

7. What we can and cannot do about abuse

7.1 What we can do

  • Block a share at our relay, using the endpoint ID in its link. Each share has a new endpoint ID, so this affects only that share.
  • Block IP addresses from using our relay, for a limited time or permanently.
  • Limit how many connections and how much data our relay accepts.
  • Report child sexual abuse material to NCMEC and to Romanian authorities, and cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities.

7.2 What we cannot do

  • See, scan, recover or delete files.
  • Identify who created a link, or give authorities records of past transfers. We keep none.
  • Revoke a link for one recipient while keeping it open for others.
  • Stop a transfer already running on a direct connection, or reach copies that recipients have saved.

7.3 Repeat infringers

If we receive repeated valid notices that the same network addresses are used to infringe copyright or for other serious abuse, we block those addresses at our relay. This is our repeat-infringer policy. Many people can share one IP address, for example on mobile networks, VPNs or workplace networks, so an address block can affect others. We use address blocks for repeated or serious abuse and review them on request.
